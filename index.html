⚡ AR Technical Interview • Q3/10
04:32
What is the primary mathematical reason quaternions are favored over Euler angles in 3D spatial tracking?
A They consume less memory on mobile GPUs
B They avoid Gimbal Lock and interpolate smoothly (SLERP)
C They require zero matrix multiplication
D They are natively executed in hardware by IMUs
✓ Correct Explanation: Quaternions represent 3D orientation without Gimbal Lock (loss of a degree of freedom when axes align) and provide seamless Spherical Linear Interpolation (SLERP) for smooth camera poses.
